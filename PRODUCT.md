# Product Context

**Product Name:** Elite Steel Concepts (ESC)
**Register:** brand

## Users
1. **Food Truck Entrepreneurs & Chefs:** Individuals starting a new mobile food business looking for reliable, custom-built, code-compliant vehicles.
2. **Business Owners & Fleet Managers:** Existing brands expanding their operations via mobile kitchens.
3. **Internal ESC Staff:** Admins managing leads, project progression, blog content, and configuration via the internal dashboard.

## Brand & Tone
- **Tone:** Confident, Industrial, Professional, Direct, Reliable.
- **Vibe:** Heavy fabrication meets premium service. It is a command center, not a toy. We deal in steel, fire, and grease, but our work is surgical and precise.

## Anti-References (What we are NOT)
- **Generic SaaS:** No airy, lightweight blue-and-white SaaS templates. 
- **AI Slop:** No gratuitous glassmorphism, no massive soft glowing orbs, no identical 6-column floating card grids.
- **Cheap & Cheerful:** We are not a budget builder. We build premium trucks, and the website must reflect high-end craftsmanship.

## Strategic Principles
- **Transparency:** Direct pricing, no bait-and-switch.
- **Speed & Precision:** Our communication is as fast and precise as our fabrication.
- **Durability:** Built to last from Day 1 to Year 10. The UI should feel as indestructible as our kitchens.
